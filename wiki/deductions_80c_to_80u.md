# Tax Deductions: Section 80C to 80U (Old Regime)

## Summary
Under the Old Tax Regime, Chapter VI-A of the Income Tax Act allows taxpayers to claim various deductions on their investments and expenditures, thereby reducing their overall taxable income.

## Details of Major Sections

### 1. Section 80C, 80CCC, 80CCD(1) - The ₹1.5 Lakh Limit
This is the most popular deduction category. The combined maximum limit for all three sections is **₹1,50,000**.
- **Section 80C:** Investments in EPF, PPF, ELSS (Mutual Funds), Life Insurance Premiums (LIC), SSY (Sukanya Samriddhi), NSC, 5-year Fixed Deposits, and tuition fees for two children.
- **Section 80CCC:** Contributions to annuity plans or pension funds of LIC/other insurers.
- **Section 80CCD(1):** Employee's contribution to the National Pension System (NPS).

### 2. Section 80CCD(1B) - Extra NPS Deduction
An additional deduction of up to **₹50,000** is available for voluntary contributions to Tier-1 NPS accounts. This is *over and above* the 80C limit of ₹1.5 Lakh.

### 3. Section 80D - Medical / Health Insurance
Deduction for health insurance premiums paid for self, family, and parents.
- **Self & Family (under 60):** Up to ₹25,000
- **Parents (under 60):** Additional ₹25,000
- **Senior Citizen Parents (60+):** Additional up to ₹50,000
- Includes up to ₹5,000 for preventive health check-ups within the overall limit.

### 4. Section 80E - Education Loan Interest
Deduction for the *interest* paid on a loan taken for higher education. 
- **Limit:** No upper limit. Can be claimed for a maximum of 8 years.

### 5. Section 80G - Donations
Deductions for donations made to charitable institutions and specific relief funds.
- Can be 100% or 50% deduction, with or without a qualifying limit, depending on the fund (e.g., PM CARES gets 100%).

### 6. Section 80DD - Disability Maintenance
Expenditure for medical treatment, training, and rehabilitation of a disabled dependent.
- **Fixed Deduction:** ₹75,000 for normal disability (40% to 80%).
- **Severe Disability:** ₹1,25,000 for severe disability (80% or more). Note: Certificate from medical authority is mandatory.

### 7. Section 80DDB - Specific Medical Treatment
Expenses for treatment of specified diseases (e.g., Cancer, Neurological diseases) for self or dependent.
- **Normal Limit:** Up to ₹40,000.
- **Senior Citizens:** Up to ₹1,00,000.

### 8. Section 80U - Personal Disability
Available to the taxpayer if they are themselves a person with a disability.
- **Fixed Deduction:** ₹75,000 (normal) or ₹1,25,000 (severe disability).

### 9. Section 80TTA & 80TTB - Bank Interest
- **Section 80TTA:** Deduction up to ₹10,000 on interest income from savings bank accounts (for individuals below 60).
- **Section 80TTB:** Deduction up to ₹50,000 on interest income from all deposits (savings + FDs) for Senior Citizens (60+).

### 10. Section 80EEA & 80EEB - Affordable Housing & EV Loans
- **Section 80EEA:** Extra deduction up to **₹1,50,000** for interest paid on a home loan for "Affordable Housing" (Stamp value up to ₹45 Lakh). This is *over and above* the Section 24(b) limit of ₹2 Lakh!
- **Section 80EEB:** Deduction up to **₹1,50,000** for interest paid on a loan taken to purchase an Electric Vehicle (EV) (loan sanctioned between 2019 to 2023).

### 11. Section 80GGC - Political Donations
- 100% deduction on any amount contributed to a registered Political Party or Electoral Trust. 
- **Rule:** Donation must NOT be in cash.

### 12. Section 80QQB & 80RRB - Royalty Income
- **Section 80QQB:** Deduction up to **₹3,00,000** on Royalty income received by authors of specific books (literary, artistic, or scientific).
- **Section 80RRB:** Deduction up to **₹3,00,000** on Royalty income on patents registered in India.

### 💡 Real Life Example
> मान लो Sneha की इनकम 8 लाख रुपये है। 
> उसने ELSS में ₹1.5 लाख इन्वेस्ट किए (**Section 80C**)।
> उसने NPS में ₹50,000 इन्वेस्ट किए (**Section 80CCD(1B)**)।
> उसने अपने और माता-पिता के लिए ₹30,000 का हेल्थ इंश्योरेंस लिया (**Section 80D**)।
> कुल Deduction = 1,50,000 + 50,000 + 30,000 = ₹2,30,000।
> अब Sneha को 8 लाख पर नहीं, बल्कि सिर्फ ₹5.7 लाख पर टैक्स देना होगा! इसे कहते हैं स्मार्ट टैक्स प्लानिंग। ✅
