# Old Tax Regime Slabs (Detailed)

## Summary
The Old Tax Regime provides taxpayers the ability to claim numerous exemptions and deductions (such as HRA, LTA, 80C, 80D, etc.) to lower their taxable income. The tax slabs in this regime vary based on the age of the taxpayer.

## Details: Tax Slabs Based on Age

### 1. Individuals below 60 years of age & HUF
This slab is applicable to regular residents, non-residents, and Hindu Undivided Families (HUF).
- **Up to ₹2,50,000:** Nil (0%)
- **₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000:** 5%
- **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000:** 20%
- **Above ₹10,00,000:** 30%

> **Note:** A tax rebate under **Section 87A** up to ₹12,500 is available, making income up to ₹5 lakh effectively tax-free in the Old Regime.

### 2. Senior Citizens (60 to 80 years of age)
Applicable to resident individuals aged 60 years or more but less than 80 years during the financial year.
- **Up to ₹3,00,000:** Nil (0%)
- **₹3,00,001 to ₹5,00,000:** 5%
- **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000:** 20%
- **Above ₹10,00,000:** 30%

### 3. Super Senior Citizens (Above 80 years of age)
Applicable to resident individuals aged 80 years or more.
- **Up to ₹5,00,000:** Nil (0%)
- **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000:** 20%
- **Above ₹10,00,000:** 30%

### 💡 Real Life Example
> मान लो Rohan (उम्र 35 साल) की सालाना आय ₹8 लाख है। 
> **Old Regime** के हिसाब से, पहले 2.5 लाख पर 0% टैक्स, अगले 2.5 लाख पर 5% (₹12,500), और बचे हुए 3 लाख पर 20% (₹60,000) टैक्स लगेगा। 
> कुल टैक्स = ₹72,500 + 4% cess। 
> लेकिन, अगर Rohan ₹1.5 लाख **80C** में और ₹50,000 **NPS (80CCD)** में निवेश करता है, तो उसकी टैक्सेबल इनकम सिर्फ ₹6 लाख रह जाएगी। इससे उसका टैक्स काफी कम हो जाएगा!
