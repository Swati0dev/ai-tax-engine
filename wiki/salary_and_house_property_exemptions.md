# Salary and House Property Exemptions (Old Regime)

## Summary
Apart from Chapter VI-A deductions, salaried employees and homeowners can claim specific exemptions under the Old Tax Regime to significantly lower their tax liability.

## Details

### 1. House Rent Allowance (HRA) - Section 10(13A)
If you live in a rented house and receive HRA as part of your salary, you can claim exemption. The exemption is the *least* of the following three:
1. Actual HRA received from the employer.
2. 50% of Basic Salary + DA (for metro cities) or 40% (for non-metros).
3. Actual rent paid minus 10% of Basic Salary + DA.

### 2. Standard Deduction
A flat deduction of **₹50,000** is available to all salaried employees and pensioners. (Note: This is available in both Old and New regimes now).

### 3. Leave Travel Allowance (LTA) - Section 10(5)
Exemption for travel expenses incurred by the employee for self and family while on leave in India. Can be claimed for 2 journeys in a block of 4 years.

### 4. Interest on Home Loan - Section 24(b)
If you have a home loan for a self-occupied property, you can claim a deduction on the *interest* component of the EMI.
- **Maximum Limit:** Up to **₹2,00,000** per financial year for a self-occupied property.
- **Key Conditions:** The loan must be taken on or after April 1, 1999, and the purchase or construction must be completed within **five years** from the end of the FY in which the loan was taken. If not completed in 5 years, the limit drops to ₹30,000.
- **Pre-Construction Interest:** Can be claimed in 5 equal annual installments starting from the year of completion.
- For let-out (rented) properties, there used to be no limit, but losses from house property that can be set off against other income are capped at ₹2,00,000.

### 5. Principal Repayment of Home Loan
The *principal* component of the home loan EMI is deductible under **Section 80C** (within the overall ₹1.5 Lakh limit).

### 💡 Real Life Example
> मान लो Vikram की सैलरी में ₹1,20,000 सालाना HRA है। वो दिल्ली (Metro) में रहता है और ₹15,000 महीना (यानी ₹1,80,000 सालाना) किराया देता है।
> उसका Basic Salary ₹4,00,000 है। 
> 1. Actual HRA = ₹1,20,000
> 2. 50% of Basic = ₹2,00,000
> 3. Rent paid - 10% of Basic = ₹1,80,000 - ₹40,000 = ₹1,40,000
> सबसे कम रकम (Least of the above) ₹1,20,000 है। 
> यानी Vikram का पूरा ₹1,20,000 HRA टैक्स-फ्री हो जाएगा! ✅ बिना किसी एक्स्ट्रा इन्वेस्टमेंट के सीधा टैक्स बच गया।
