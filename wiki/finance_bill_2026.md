# Finance Bill 2026 Updates

## Summary
The Finance Bill 2026 brings historic changes, most notably the transition to the brand new **Income-tax Act, 2025** replacing the old 1961 Act. It also increases the basic tax-free limit in the new regime to ₹12 lakh and introduces changes to corporate taxation and TCS.

## Details

### 1. New Income-tax Act, 2025
Effective April 1, 2026, the old Income-tax Act of 1961 is being replaced by the new Income-tax Act, 2025. The goal is to simplify tax structures and make compliance easier for everyday taxpayers.

### 2. Personal Income Tax Changes
- **Higher Exemption:** The tax-free income limit in the New Regime has been increased to **₹12 lakh**.
- **Regime Choice:** The dual-regime system continues. You can still choose the Old Regime if you want deductions like **Section 80C**.

### 3. Business & Corporate Tax
- **Share Buy-backs:** Now taxed as capital gains.
- **Data Centers & IFSC:** Huge tax holidays (up to 2047) for foreign companies providing data center services in India.

### 4. Other Reliefs
- **TCS Reduction:** Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on overseas tour packages and education/medical remittances under LRS is reduced to 2%.

### 💡 Real Life Example
> मान लो Amit अपने बेटे को पढ़ाई के लिए विदेश भेज रहा है और पैसे रेमिट (remit) कर रहा है। 
> पहले TCS (Tax Collected at Source) ज़्यादा कटता था, जिससे तुरंत बहुत सारा पैसा ब्लॉक हो जाता था। 
> **Finance Bill 2026** के बाद, education के लिए LRS रेमिटेंस पर TCS घटाकर सिर्फ **2%** कर दिया गया है। यानी Amit का कैश फ्लो अब फँसेगा नहीं, और वो आराम से पैसे भेज सकता है। ✅
