# CONTENT_VALIDATION_WORKFLOW.md - Content Validation Workflow

Read this file before publishing, editing, or using tax explanation content in pages, search, chat, or examples.

## Validation Flow

1. Identify the content record, page, chat answer, or example being validated.
2. Compare it with structured tax data and source references.
3. Check that the tax category, law, section, form, procedure, and user applicability are correct.
4. Confirm that exact numbers, deadlines, thresholds, and rates have source support.
5. Confirm that uncertain claims are marked clearly.
6. Confirm that illegal evasion content is refused or removed.
7. Update review status and notes.

## Required Checks

- No invented tax sections, forms, rules, rates, thresholds, penalties, or due dates.
- No universal claims when facts depend on income type, turnover, residency, registration status, financial year, or assessment year.
- No unofficial source is treated as final authority.
- No draft or outdated record is presented as verified.
- Plain-language explanations preserve legal caution.
- Related sections and forms are relevant and not merely decorative.

## Validation Result

Use one of these outcomes:

- `verified`: Supported by official or project-approved sources.
- `needs-review`: Plausible but incomplete, conflicting, or awaiting official confirmation.
- `outdated`: Previously valid but no longer reliable for current use.
- `draft`: Not ready for user-facing authoritative guidance.
