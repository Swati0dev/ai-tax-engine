# RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md - Tax Research Workflow

Read this file before researching tax law, forms, sections, rates, rules, circulars, notifications, due dates, penalties, or procedures.

## Research Flow

1. Define the exact claim or user question being researched.
2. Identify the tax category: Direct Tax, Indirect Tax, GST, form, procedure, or unknown.
3. Search official government sources first.
4. Cross-check important claims with another official or project-approved source when possible.
5. Capture the source title, URL, publication or update date when available, and access date.
6. Mark the content as `verified`, `needs-review`, `outdated`, or `draft`.
7. Summarize only what is relevant to the task.

## Official Source Priority

- Income Tax Department e-Filing portal.
- Income Tax Department forms, instructions, utilities, and help pages.
- Central Board of Direct Taxes resources.
- GST portal.
- Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs resources.
- Official acts, rules, circulars, notifications, and government help pages.

## Cross-Checking Rules

- Exact rates, thresholds, deadlines, penalties, and forms need official confirmation.
- If official sources conflict, record the conflict and mark the record as `needs-review`.
- If only unofficial sources are found, use them for orientation only and do not mark the claim verified.
- Confirm financial year, assessment year, effective date, or notification date when relevant.

## Research Output

Every research result should include:

- Claim researched.
- Short answer or finding.
- Source references.
- Date relevance.
- Verification status.
- Remaining uncertainty or follow-up.
