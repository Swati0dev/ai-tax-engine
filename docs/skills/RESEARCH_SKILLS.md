# RESEARCH_SKILLS.md - Research Behavior for Agents

Read this file before researching tax law, forms, rates, rules, circulars, procedures, or official guidance.

## Research Principles

- Prefer official government sources first.
- Do not rely on a single source when a claim is important.
- Cross-check tax law, forms, due dates, thresholds, rates, and procedures with multiple trusted sources when possible.
- Verify that information is relevant to India and the correct tax category.
- Confirm the effective date, assessment year, financial year, or notification date when applicable.

## Source Priority

1. Official government portals and tax department websites.
2. Official acts, rules, circulars, notifications, and forms.
3. Government help pages and official user guides.
4. Reputable secondary sources only for orientation.

## After Research

- Extract only important points.
- Summarize in short, clear language.
- Preserve source links and dates.
- Mark uncertain or incomplete information as needs-review.
- Avoid unnecessary detail that does not help the user task.

## When Research Is Insufficient

If verified information cannot be found:

- Say the claim is not verified.
- Do not guess.
- Add a task or note for future verification.
- Recommend checking official sources or consulting a qualified professional.
