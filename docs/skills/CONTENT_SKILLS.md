# CONTENT_SKILLS.md - Tax Content Writing Behavior

Read this file before writing tax explanations, examples, page content, or chat response copy.

## Writing Goals

- Be clear, concise, and legally careful.
- Explain tax concepts in plain language.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions.
- Avoid overclaiming certainty.
- Help users understand lawful compliance and legal tax-saving options.

## Standard Explanation Pattern

Use this pattern for tax content:

1. What it means.
2. Who it applies to.
3. Conditions or limits.
4. Related forms or procedures.
5. Practical example.
6. Legal tax-saving options, if any.
7. Caveats and source status.

## Chat Answer Pattern

Chat answers should include:

1. Short answer.
2. Likely tax category.
3. Applicable section, law, or concept.
4. Applicability conditions.
5. Relevant forms or procedures.
6. Legal tax-saving options, if any.
7. Example or scenario explanation.
8. Caveats and when to consult a professional.
9. Sources or data status.

## Prohibited Content

- Illegal evasion guidance.
- Fake deductions or invoice strategies.
- Advice to hide income or misreport facts.
- Unverified law presented as final authority.
- Guarantees of outcomes.
