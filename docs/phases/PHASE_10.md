# PHASE_10.md - Maintenance and Content Expansion

Objective: Keep tax content accurate and the project maintainable over time.

## Phase 10.1 - Content Expansion

Tasks:

- Add more direct tax sections.
- Add more GST and indirect tax topics.
- Add examples and form connections.
- Keep new content source-backed or clearly marked as draft.

Deliverables:

- Expanded tax data.
- Updated indexes and related links.

## Phase 10.2 - Periodic Review

Tasks:

- Review tax content against official sources.
- Update outdated records.
- Log review activity.
- Confirm effective dates, financial years, assessment years, or notification dates where relevant.

Deliverables:

- Updated review status.
- Logged source review history.

## Phase 10.3 - Documentation and Workflow Maintenance

Tasks:

- Update project reference files when architecture or behavior changes.
- Keep decisions, known issues, and learnings current.
- Remove obsolete references instead of creating duplicate guidance.

Deliverables:

- Current project documentation.
- Updated improvement log when better approaches are discovered.
